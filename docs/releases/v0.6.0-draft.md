# transit v0.6.0 (draft)

Draft release notes for v0.6.0, the instruments release. Keep this file in step with what lands; it becomes `v0.6.0.md` at the tag. transit is alpha-quality software and the session format may still change, so back up anything you care about.

v0.5.0 shipped the Macro Oscillator (24 models, concert pitch, 1 to 16 voices with a note-to-macro mapping), clip tools and fades, browser preview, Touch mode, the Info panel, editor windows that follow the main window, Linux plug-in editors and disk-backed long takes ([v0.5.0 notes](v0.5.0.md)). What is new here is racks, the second ported module, MIDI effects, the Breaking change to the default instrument below, and the other items listed.

## Highlights

- **Breaking: the Subtractive Synth is gone and the Macro Oscillator is the default instrument.** A new instrument track now carries the Macro Oscillator (default model, 8 voices). The built-in Subtractive Synth is removed, with its panel, presets and help. A session saved with it opens with a Macro Oscillator in its place: the old synth's parameters, and the MIDI mappings, automation lanes and rack macro targets that pointed at them, are dropped, the load report says what was dropped, and the status line reads "N track(s) used the retired Subtractive Synth and now carry the Macro Oscillator". The session opens as modified so the substitution shows on save. Notes, clips, the track's mixer settings, racks and inserts are untouched, but the track sounds different until you pick a patch. Levels may differ: the Macro Oscillator plays louder than the old synth did, so substituted tracks get a lowered level (-19.5 dB) to land near the old loudness; adjust it to taste (#234, [ADR 0033](https://github.com/transit-audio/transit/blob/main/docs/adr/0033-built-in-instruments-mutable-instruments-port.md)).
- **Filter insert with note-triggered envelopes.** A new built-in insert, Filter: a resonant low, high, band or notch filter (12 or 24 dB, with drive) whose ADSR plays from the track's notes after the MIDI effects, at the note's exact sample, paraphonically (one envelope for all held notes, Every note or Legato retrigger), plus an LFO (free or tempo-synced), an envelope follower, key tracking and an optional amp envelope. It works after any instrument, built-in or hosted. Presets Pluck, Wah and Paraphonic Brass (#242, [ADR 0038](https://github.com/transit-audio/transit/blob/main/docs/adr/0038-inserts-read-the-tracks-note-stream.md)).
- **Macro Oscillator: per-voice shaper and modulation matrix.** An optional section after each voice's low-pass gate, transit's addition and off by default: an amp envelope with velocity, a low, band or high pass filter (12 or 24 dB, drive, key tracking) with its own envelope, and two LFOs (six shapes, free or tempo-synced and locked to the song position, retrigger or free-running, fade-in). A 16-row modulation matrix routes LFOs, envelopes, velocity, key, release velocity, aftertouch, poly aftertouch, the mod wheel, pitch bend, a per-note random value and, on a rack member, the rack's eight macros to pitch, the macros, decay, level, cutoff, resonance, the filter envelope amount, the LFOs' rates and amounts and pan, each row with a curve and a via source; every row is an automation target. **Breaking / Migration:** the note-to-macro grid is gone: sessions and presets that used it open with its depths as matrix rows and sound the same, bit for bit (a session that never used it opens unmodified). A session saved by this version loses its matrix rows and shaper settings when opened in v0.5.x, which ignores the parameters it does not know, and the old grid's depths are not there either (#243, [ADR 0033](https://github.com/transit-audio/transit/blob/main/docs/adr/0033-built-in-instruments-mutable-instruments-port.md)).
- **Take lanes: see the stacked takes, choose the one that plays, flatten.** After cycle recording (or recording over clips) a track's takes show as lanes under it, newest on top, with the newest take playing, and the rest of the track keeps playing (before, a cycle recording left the rest of the track silent, and an audio cycle recording cut away the clip under the loop). Click a take's clip in its lane to play that take there, or its label to play it wherever it was recorded; choosing a longer take back makes it whole again. Flatten Takes keeps what plays and removes the other takes; Delete Take removes a take that does not play. The T button on the track header and the track menu show or hide the lanes. Each change is one undo step, and a frozen track refuses them (#25, [ADR 0039](https://github.com/transit-audio/transit/blob/main/docs/adr/0039-takes-and-comping.md)).
  Sessions recorded with an earlier build now restore their cycle laps as numbered takes on open, with the newest playing and one **Migrate takes** undo step; frozen tracks stay unchanged and are reported. A hand-made arrangement whose active playlist holds a single trimmed or re-placed variant over a clip on an earlier playlist is read the same way and migrates too; the step is one undo away and the load report names the track (#249).
- **Texture insert.** A new built-in insert effect, Texture: a port of Émilie Gillet's texture synthesizer firmware (MIT), bit-exact against her C++ at every buffer quality. It records its input and plays it back as clouds of grains (time, size, shape and pitch, each with its own randomness; density, feedback, dry/wet, reverb), freezes, seeds grains from a button or an automatable trigger, and turns into a tape delay with Size at the top. Each quality (Digital, Vintage Digital, Clean Tape, Dirty Tape) runs at the module's own rate, converted at the device's edges, so a 48 kHz session with Digital or Clean Tape hears the firmware's samples unchanged; the latency goes to delay compensation. Buffer 4 to 8 seconds; the module's automatic input gain on by default. Presets Granular Cloud, Frozen Pad and Tape Delay (#241, [ADR 0033](https://github.com/transit-audio/transit/blob/main/docs/adr/0033-built-in-instruments-mutable-instruments-port.md)).

## Fixes

- A note-off that lands on its own note-on's sample (a zero-length note in a sequenced or imported clip, or a same-sample retrigger behind a moved release) is now heard one sample after the note-on on every path, offline render included, so no note is zero-length (#257). The diagnostics strip rows count the skips a strip takes before its first send (#256).
