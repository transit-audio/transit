  • Pads is where most people will live: a 4×4 grid per bank, the selected pad's waveform and six controls beside it. The right strip is the whole per-pad surface; anything deeper opens the shaper and matrix the Macro Oscillator already has.
  • Slices keeps the loop on a bench: the source picker is the only new concept (file cue points first, then transients, then grid), and two buttons move the result to pads or to a MIDI clip.
  • Instrument is the key map: zones with velocity splits and round robins, one zone strip, loop rules as the ADR fixed them. SFZ import lands here.
  • Not in the panel on purpose: per-pad effects or outputs, a mixer page, modulation pages. Expand to Rack is the door for anyone who needs that.
