Anatomy
Top-left pad id, bank + slot (A6), Mono 10 text.faint. Replaces the bare number: groups cross banks, so the bank must show. Top-right choke badge: the group number, Mono 500 9 in a 14 px box, 1 px line.strong, text.muted. Absent when Off. Top edge the group's colour as a 3 px tag, only if the group has one. Selecting a grouped pad outlines its peers (1 px dashed text.muted, inset 3) and dots any other bank that holds one. Choked a pad cut by its group drops its hit fill at once and its badge inverts (text.muted fill) for 200 ms: you see who cut whom. Bottom-right M (text fill, inverted) or S (signal fill) chip. Muted pads 40%; while any pad is solo, the others dim to 40% too.
