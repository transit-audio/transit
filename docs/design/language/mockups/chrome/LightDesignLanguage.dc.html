One panel language for every device

Graphite surfaces, one signal colour for what you set, one modulation colour for what moves it. Every device is built from the same panels, knobs, displays and routing rows.

01 Show the sound Every section leads with a display of what it does: waveform, curve, envelope, response.
02 Two colours mean two things Blue is a value you set. Amber is modulation acting on it. Nothing else is coloured.
03 Values are always readable Every control prints its value in mono, in real units. No hover needed to know a setting.
04 Panels, not chrome Flat raised panels on a graphite ground. No skeuomorphic metal, glow or gradients.

Fader · Toggle · Pad

sends · crossfade · layer mix
One dB taper everywhere: +6 at the top, unity at 14% of travel, ~85% of travel above −24, −∞ compressed at the bottom. Fill in signal from the bottom to the cap; muted strips fill in ghost. Fader, scale and meter share that taper so positions line up. Meter: peak at 35%, RMS solid, 2 px peak hold, red only above 0 dBFS. Drag, Shift fine, Alt/double-click to unity, click readout to type.
Toggle
Role Off On {{t.role}} Section enable
Latching buttons: off is raised and muted; on fills with the role colour. Mute inverts to light, Solo is signal, Arm is hot red, Monitor and option toggles get a signal outline. Unavailable reads ghost, not hidden. The switch is only for enabling a whole section.
Pad
{{p.state}}
Square, radius 6, raised. Number top-left, note top-right, sample name bottom-left. A hit fills from the bottom with signal at 26%, height = velocity, with a solid edge, decaying over ~250 ms. Selected = 2 px signal border. Optional 3 px colour tag on top for groups or choke. Empty pads are dashed and accept a dropped sample.

Device anatomy

A · TITLE BARTrack / device, preset browser centred, output meter, bypass, menu. Identical on every device.
B · HEROThe device’s main display plus its 2–4 most important controls: oscillator scope, sample waveform, EQ curve, transfer curve.
C · SECTIONSPanels on a 12-column grid. Each panel: section label, optional display well, a row of knobs.
D · ROUTINGSource chips plus routing rows, full width at the bottom. Only for devices that modulate.
