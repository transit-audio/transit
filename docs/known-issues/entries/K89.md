With the render workers on (the default), a plug-in that hangs inside a render worker (never returns from `process`) keeps its strip away for good: every session change is refused while it is away (ADR 0029 deviation 12; counted as deferred publications), its track stays silent, and the UI says so with an Unresponsive marker (#190, #213) on the insert row, the device card and the arrange track header of every hosted plug-in on that track once the worker has held the strip for 2 s; the engine cannot say which plug-in on the track hangs, so the hover text says a plug-in on the track and offers Reconnect. The Render worker figures (Strip overruns, busy) keep rising as before.
