Native WASAPI exclusive mode on the Focusrite Scarlett 2i2: the driver reports a 3 ms minimum period but delivers events every other period below its 10 ms default (992 xruns in 6 s at 144 frames, 213 at 256), and the round trip stays about 220 ms at 480 frames, so the latency is inside the Focusrite WDM driver in every WASAPI mode on this device; ASIO is the low-latency path there.
