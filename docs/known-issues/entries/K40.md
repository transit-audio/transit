A session with a looped clip ([ADR 0019](../../adr/0019-clip-internal-loops.md)) that an older build opens and saves loses the loop (the unknown field is dropped); builds from the `needs` marker on warn on load about features they do not know, the ones before it cannot, leaving a long clip that is silent past its source. A MIDI loop cycle is a fixed span of samples, so a tempo change inside a looped clip drifts later cycles off the bar grid. A tempo change that would squeeze a loop under 64 samples is refused, naming the clip.
