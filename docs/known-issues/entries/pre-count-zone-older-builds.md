Sessions with a pre-count zone in older builds (#441, ADR 0052 decision 13). Recording with notes played during the count-in from bar 1 puts a MIDI clip before bar 1. A build from before v0.7.3 loads the session but cannot reach that part: its ruler and Return to Zero stop at bar 1, so the pickups do not play, and its bounce leaves them out. Saving without touching the clip keeps it whole, but moving it (or anything that moves every clip) puts its start on bar 1 and shifts its notes later by the zone's length. Builds since the session marker warn when they open such a session, because it lists the `pre_count` need; builds before the marker do not warn. A session saved by v0.7.3 with a structured unplaced-take entry (#705) cannot be opened at all by v0.4.1 through v0.7.2: they report "not a transit session: invalid type: map, expected a string" (builds before v0.4.1 open it without the unplaced-take list); such entries list takes recorded wholly before bar 1, on tracks deleted while recording, with playlists that could not be copied, or with unreadable, damaged or too-long files. Sessions with no unplaced takes still open in older builds, subject to the pre-count limits above.
