Parameter smoothing (#279): with variable block sizes, a parameter smoother's `advance(n)` snaps to its target when a short block (1 to 8 frames, such as the tail after the EQ's 32-frame chunks) cannot move the value, even though a longer block would. The jump is at most the f32 stall distance of one sample. Worst measured: 1.9e-4 of the EQ range at 192 kHz (about 1 Hz at 19 kHz at 48 kHz) and 1.5e-3 of the reverb size at 192 kHz (a 0.19 percent delay-length step that might tick in a tail). Rare: it needs a tiny block late in a settle at a high rate.
