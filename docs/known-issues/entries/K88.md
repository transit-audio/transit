Mixer strips render on several cores by default (#124, [ADR 0029](../../adr/0029-strip-job-slots.md)), since 2026-10-29: start transit with `TRANSIT_RENDER_POOL=0` to render every strip serially on the audio thread instead (the opt-out; `1` or unset is the default, anything else is logged and ignored). The strips' deadline is 150% of the period for device periods of 1.5 ms and below (64 frames at 48 kHz) and 100% above (#172; `TRANSIT_STRIP_DEADLINE_PERCENT` overrides it for measurement); a strip whose render misses the deadline is silent for that block (counted under Render worker figures). A session with sidechain keys renders through the render workers as a task graph by default (#174 slice 1, since 2026-10-29); start transit with `TRANSIT_RENDER_GRAPH=0` to render such a session serially on the audio thread instead (the opt-out; `1` or unset is the default, anything else is logged and ignored). Strips that read the live input or the hardware (monitored audio tracks, External Instruments, hardware inserts) render on the audio thread; the aux and bus tail renders through the graph with `TRANSIT_RENDER_TAIL=1` (opt-in); the serial tail stays the default until the paired measurement. Masters stay on the audio thread after the tail. The graph opt-out wins; live auxes count toward the four-strip threshold, and only an away tail strip forces the tail recovery path. A strip keyed from a source that stays suspect keeps its meter frozen and gets its queued MIDI late, all at once, when the source recovers (as a suspect strip itself does on the plain path). While a strip is held by a late worker, the MIDI Editor's audition note (`live_note`) for it is dropped, its instrument's event counters are left out of the totals, and a session change waits until the strip is back (a worker that never returns delays it until the device is closed).
