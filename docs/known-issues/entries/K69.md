Native JACK (ADR 0021): a server quantum or rate change while the device runs reopens it (the transport stops; a take is kept). Under PipeWire's JACK library that happens when another program forces a quantum, when a forcing client leaves, when the node moves to another driver or when the driver resumes; PipeWire does not change the quantum just because another client asks for a smaller one, since every JACK client locks the quantum by default and only a forced quantum overrides the lock (#760, ADR 0021 amendment, rule 7). The rate and buffer size are now taken from the active client, and a chosen buffer size that arrives within half a second of opening is the device's, so neither reopens the device after its start (#760, slice 1); a graph rate other than the one transit prepared for costs one rebuild before any audio. Not yet confirmed on a PipeWire machine.
