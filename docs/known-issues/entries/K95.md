Macro Oscillator: raising **Voices** from 1 while several keys are held legato leaves them all on voice 0. Playing one of those held keys again (not the newest) re-strikes it on voice 0 and drops the other held keys there: they fall silent though still held, and their note-offs do nothing. Nothing sounds stuck. Reachable only by changing the voice count mid-phrase from mono (#196).
