At a converted device rate (the device runs at a rate other than the session's), starting Play or Record from a stop cut audio that was already sounding (#725). Since v0.7.4 Play and Record without a count-in keep the converter running through the start (confirmed by the maintainer on one setup, a 44.1 kHz interface with a 48 kHz session). Three limits remain, accepted. Record with a count-in restarts the converter when the count-in begins: about 17 to 19 session frames (0.35 to 0.4 ms) are skipped and the filter starts from silence, on the output frame where the first click starts; the first click's timing does not move. On that same setup, with a monitored guitar chord ringing, the maintainer heard no cut. Locate while playing discards the pending audio at once and restarts the converter, so audio sounding across a locate is cut. At equal rates, after a half-speed play runs to its end, monitoring while stopped reads through the filter half a frame late until the next start. A device at the session rate is unaffected by the first two.
