A recorded take longer than the decode limit was not placed. Sources lived fully decoded in memory, so the decoder refuses a file over 1 Gi samples (`MAX_DECODE_SAMPLES` in `crates/audio-io/src/pcm.rs`): 6.2 h of mono at 48 kHz, 3.1 h of stereo. Since issue #187 (slice 1, [ADR 0035](../../adr/0035-disk-backed-audio-sources.md)) a take over the limit, or over the stream setting (10 minutes by default), is placed as one clip played from its file, and takes an older build left unplaced are placed when the session opens. What remains: a take that cannot be played from disk (a compressed file, or a sample rate other than the session's) and is too long to decode is still refused as #192 made it: a WARN line, a message naming the file, its length and the limit in hours, and an entry under the session's unplaced takes.
