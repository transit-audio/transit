A recorded take longer than the decode limit (`MAX_DECODE_SAMPLES` in `crates/audio-io/src/pcm.rs`: 1 Gi samples, 6.2 h of mono at 48 kHz, 3.1 h of stereo) that cannot be played from disk (a compressed file, or a sample rate other than the session's) is refused and not placed: a WARN line, a message naming the file, its length and the limit in hours, and an entry under the session's unplaced takes. A PCM take at the session rate is placed as one clip played from its file ([ADR 0035](../../adr/0035-disk-backed-audio-sources.md)).
