DAWproject import is slice 1 of #261: devices (VST3, CLAP, built-in), audio clips, sends, group, effect and VCA tracks, parameter automation, linked clips and anything timed in seconds are left out; the open lists each kind with a count. Per-note timbre becomes channel CC74 owned by the most recently started sounding note, which is lossy for chords; other per-note expressions (gain, pan, transpose, formant) are dropped. Tempo and controller ramps become steps at their points. Group tracks are counted and their member tracks import ungrouped. Hostile files are refused or capped: a `project.xml` over 64 MB or with more than 1 million `<` or 3 million `=` (what the XML parser reserves room for before parsing), more than 64 levels of nesting or 64 attributes on an element, 1,000 tracks, 10,000 clips, 1,000 markers, 10,000 tempo or meter changes, 4 million notes and controller events.
