On an audio device whose input and output run in one callback (native ASIO, JACK) at 1x, transit aligns the live input to the output exactly, so a hardware insert round trip or External Instrument latency measured at one device rate stays sample-aligned at another: the rate converters' part is recomputed for the rate in use. Three limits remain, by design. A measurement used at another device rate than it was taken at still says "measure again at this rate", because the interface's own delay can change with the rate. With input and output on separate streams (WASAPI, cpal, or an input on another device) the converters' delay is not deterministic: a figure is used as measured and says "measure again at this rate" whenever the device rate or the input timing differs from the measurement's. After the transport plays at another speed with the device at another rate than the session's, the timing is not restored until a stop (or locate) at 1x (at the session's own rate the input is read exactly as before and the "timing changed" hints never show): until then measured figures are used as measured and say "timing changed: stop at 1x, then measure again", and a manual External Instrument latency says "timing changed: stop at 1x, then check the manual latency" and is never changed. A figure saved by v0.7.4 or older says "measure again at this rate" at every rate until it is measured again.
