System Audio (#268): a lasting offset of the input callbacks' arrival times of about 1 ms or less (a device that loses up to about 48 frames at 48 kHz without filling or flagging them, or a shift of the typical callback delay) is within the device line's 1 ms band, so the lower-envelope fit spans the step for one window instead of restarting: measured at the estimator (200 s, the offset from 60 s), 1 ms with no jitter reads 90 ppm off at 128 and 480 frames for 18 s, 46 ppm at 960 for 27 s (55 s when the offset also ends) and 25 ppm at 8192 for 27 s; 0.5 ms reads 34 to 40 ppm for 9 to 14 s; 0.2 ms reads within 17 ppm. Offsets from 1.2 ms up restart the line when they begin and when they end and read within 20 ppm. Native WASAPI fills a discontinuity with zeros counted from the device position and native ASIO invalidates the next stamp on a position jump (which restarts the line), so only a backend that does neither (cpal), a WASAPI timestamp error at the gap, or a change in the host's wake latency reaches it.
