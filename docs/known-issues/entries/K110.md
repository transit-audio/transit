The Sampler's core path (#269, C2) holds every zone's audio in memory: a sample longer than 30 s or 32 MiB of decoded audio, or one over the session's streaming threshold, is refused for a zone, and a patch holds at most 128 MiB of distinct audio. Its zone controls (`zone001_level`, ...) are not automatable yet.
