TASCAM Model 12 WASAPI exclusive playback measures about 270 ms round trip (277 ms at 441 frames, 271 ms at 256) while the driver reports a 10 ms stream latency, a buffer equal to the period and 0 xruns, regardless of period; shared mode measures 60 to 73 ms. The extra delay follows the render stream (exclusive render with shared capture 301 ms; shared render with exclusive capture 54 ms), so exclusive capture is unaffected, and on some opens no audio passes (one of two shared-render, exclusive-capture runs heard no click). The input endpoint also reported 48000 Hz against the unit's pinned 44.1 kHz in every run. Like K25's period behavior on the Scarlett, this is a driver-specific exclusive-mode quirk.
