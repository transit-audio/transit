A lost graphics device (a driver reset, a GPU power-state change, #104) closes transit instead of re-creating the device: the frame loop takes the bounded shutdown (autosave kept, plug-ins shut down, a message box) rather than freezing. egui-wgpu 0.36 and eframe 0.36 have no device re-creation: egui-wgpu's buffer upload (`update_buffers`) panics on a lost device and its surface "Lost" recovery runs after that upload and only re-creates the surface. A loss first seen inside that upload still reaches the shutdown through the panic path.
