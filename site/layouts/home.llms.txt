# transit

> transit is a free, open-source DAW (digital audio workstation) for recording, editing and mixing music on Windows, Mac and Linux. It is alpha software: it changes every week, and the session format may still change.

transit records audio and MIDI with stacked takes, edits in an arrangement and a piano roll, and mixes with buses, sends, sidechains, automation and plug-in delay compensation. It hosts CLAP and VST3 plug-ins and includes its own instruments (a Sampler with pads, slices and key zones, and synths ported from Émilie Gillet's MIT-licensed firmware) and effects. On Windows it talks to ASIO and WASAPI directly; on Linux to JACK and PipeWire. It is licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0.

The features page separates what is in transit now (Shipped) from planned work (Coming, with no dates promised).
